Key Concept ID: BIO-03-A

Glycolysis: Cytoplasmic Cleavage

Glycolysis is the anaerobic breakdown of one 6-carbon glucose molecule into two 3-carbon pyruvate molecules.

Energy Investment: Consumes 2 ATP to phosphorylate glucose.
Energy Harvest: Yields 4 ATP + 2 NADH (Net: +2 ATP).
No cap: AP Exam Trap! ⚠️ Glycolysis requires no oxygen and occurs in the cytosol, NOT inside mitochondria!
Formula Highlight ID: BIO-03-B

Aerobic Respiration Equation

C₆H₁₂O₆ + 6O₂ → 6CO₂ + 6H₂O + 30-32 ATP
